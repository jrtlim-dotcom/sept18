My Autobiography

My name is Jensen Ryan T. Lim. I am an educator whose professional journey has been shaped by my passion for teaching, technology, continuous learning, and service to learners and fellow educators.

My journey in education has allowed me to experience different dimensions of teaching and learning. From information and communications technology to technical-vocational education, I have continuously explored ways to make education relevant to the needs of learners and the changing demands of society.

I currently serve as a Master Teacher I at Lopez National Comprehensive High School, where I am involved in the Senior High School Department. My responsibilities have allowed me to work not only as a classroom teacher but also as a coordinator, subject group leader, trainer, and learning facilitator.

As an educator, I believe that teaching is more than delivering lessons. It is about understanding learners, developing their skills, encouraging discipline, providing meaningful learning experiences, and preparing them for life beyond the classroom.

My Professional Journey

Discovering My Passion for Technology

My professional interests developed around technology and information and communications technology. I became interested in how computers, networking, digital tools, and emerging technologies could solve problems and create opportunities.

Entering the Teaching Profession

My journey as an educator gave me the opportunity to transform my technical knowledge into meaningful learning experiences. Teaching allowed me to share knowledge while continuously learning from my students.

Growing as a Senior High School Teacher

In Senior High School, I became particularly involved in Technical-Vocational-Livelihood education and ICT. I have worked with learners in areas related to computer systems servicing, digital technologies, productivity applications, and other technology-based competencies.

Taking Leadership Responsibilities

As my career developed, I took on additional responsibilities in school leadership and coordination. These experiences strengthened my skills in planning, organizing programs, coordinating teachers, facilitating professional learning, and supporting school improvement.

Becoming a Lifelong Learner

I pursued advanced studies because I believe that educators should never stop learning. Graduate studies, professional development programs, training, research, and collaboration with other educators have contributed greatly to my continuing growth.
